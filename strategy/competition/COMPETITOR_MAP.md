# Competitor Map

## Buckets

1. Generic format converters (high SEO surface, low specialization)
   - Convertio
   - OnlineConverter

2. AI/OCR semantic converters (higher compute cost, better reconstructed text)
   - pdf2epub.ai
   - papers-to-markdown style pipelines

3. Source-native converters (not PDF-layout-preserving)
   - arXiv HTML/pandoc workflows

4. Reflow tooling (adjacent but not full web product strategy)
   - k2pdfopt

## Your Positioning

High-fidelity paper reading conversion focused on preserving visual structure, figures, formulas, and reading flow for e-readers.

## Why This Is Defensible

- Better fit for difficult academic layouts where OCR order can fail.
- Less post-conversion proofreading burden for users.
- Clear target user: people reading technical papers on small devices.

## Messaging Angle

"Not just PDF->EPUB conversion; paper-native reading optimization for e-readers."

## Practical Differentiators To Highlight

- Two-column handling quality
- Formula and figure continuity
- Predictable output for complex paper layouts
- Open-source transparency and reproducibility
