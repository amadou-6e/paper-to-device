# SEO + AdSense Experiment Plan

## Objective

Test whether a paper-focused converter website can attract qualified organic clicks and generate early ad revenue signal.

## Key Principle

Use SEO pages to attract traffic; reserve expensive processing for controlled free tier or paid jobs.

## 60-Day Experiment

### Phase 1 (Days 1-14): Foundation

- Publish website with fast landing pages.
- Add pages:
  - Home (problem + solution)
  - "PDF to EPUB for Academic Papers"
  - "Best way to read arXiv papers on e-readers"
  - "Two-column PDF to EPUB without broken reading order"
  - Comparison pages (vs OCR-first, vs generic converters)
- Add Search Console and analytics.
- Prepare for AdSense eligibility (privacy, terms, contact, enough content).

### Phase 2 (Days 15-35): SEO Content Build

- Publish 8 to 12 high-intent pages.
- Internal link every page to one core conversion page.
- Add before/after examples for real papers.
- Add FAQ sections with direct query phrasing.

### Phase 3 (Days 36-60): AdSense + Optimization

- Enable AdSense once traffic and content quality are sufficient.
- Start with conservative placements:
  - one in-content unit after intro
  - one right-rail unit on desktop
  - one low-impact mobile unit
- Measure RPM, CTR, bounce rate, and conversion starts.

## KPI Targets

- 1,000+ organic sessions/month by day 60
- 3% to 8% landing-page to "try conversion" click rate
- Ad RPM signal > $3 on informational pages
- First 10 to 30 user uploads from organic + social

## Guardrails

- Do not overload pages with ads before trust is established.
- Keep page speed high.
- Keep legal pages and data policy clear.
- Keep compute-heavy conversions rate-limited for free users.

## Decision Gate at Day 60

Continue if:
- organic trend is increasing
- useful click-through to product exists
- ad signal is non-trivial on content pages

Pivot if:
- traffic stays flat despite consistent content
- ad revenue cannot offset basic hosting/ops
- users only want one-off free conversions with high compute cost
